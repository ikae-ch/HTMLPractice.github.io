susquatches evolution is pretty interesting. They had started as a little aggressive indie rock band during 1st demo and Plum/Ceto. They had
gotten a little calmer during Water Plant, and moved to a jazzy math rock during the duration of Weathering and In This World. Towards the
end of their career, in TOY BOX BRAIN and 4th, they had moved to a classic indie rock with traces of the jazzy formula from earlier releases.

If you are interested in getting into susquatch, I believe the best places to start are:
Water Plant (EP) - While, to me, this EP doesn't have any standout tracks, it does have some really strong ones, such as polka, and harvest
In This World (Album) - Where I started. It is their best work, in my opinion, but leaves you a little jaded when listening to their other work. If you had to pick one song, start with Spin The Words
Plum/Ceto (Single) - Great for a short listen. Contains my personal favourite track, "Ceto". It is a little more aggressive compared to their other works, especially Ceto, which is why I wouldn't recommend it. It can leave a different impression on what susquatch's music may sound like.
