On Their Evolution

susquatch's evolution is pretty interesting. They had started as a slightly aggressive indie rock band during 1st demo and Plum/Ceto. They had gotten a little calmer during Water Plant, and moved to a jazzy math rock during the duration of Weathering and In This World. Towards the end of their career, in TOY BOX BRAIN and 4th, they had moved to a classic indie rock with traces of the jazzy formula from earlier releases.

Where To Start

Water Plant (EP) While, to me, this EP doesn't have any standout tracks, it does have some really strong ones, such as polka, and harvest.
In This World (Album) Where I started. It is their best work, in my opinion, but leaves you a little jaded when listening to their other work. If you had to pick one song, start with Spin The Words.
Plum/Ceto (Single) Great for a short listen. Contains my personal favourite track, "Ceto". It is a little more aggressive compared to their other works, which is why I wouldn't recommend it first. It can leave a different impression on what susquatch's music may sound like.
